1 How to Login & System Authentication

The Hurix Time Tracker web application runs on any desktop or mobile browser. It features dual authentication mechanisms to guarantee instant access while maintaining enterprise data security.

Method A: Automatic Google SSO (Recommended)

When accessing the tool from your official Google Workspace profile (e.g. arun.prakash@hurix.ai), the web app auto-detects your active session.

  • Zero Password Entry: Automatically authenticates in under 1 second.
  • Loads your pre-configured team, work mode, and client assignments instantly.
Method B: Work Email & Password Fallback

If accessing via incognito mode, a guest browser, or if SSO is not detected, the manual login card appears:

  1. Enter your registered corporate email (e.g. user.name@hurix.ai).
  2. Enter your password (system initial default: Hurix@123).
  3. Click Sign In with Password.
Role-Based Navigation: Associates access the User Portal. Authorized Team Leads and Managers also receive the Live Shift Monitor button, and Executive Leadership receives access to Executive Analytics.
2 Daily Shift Lifecycle: How to Clock In, Take Breaks & Clock Out
Employee Workflow
2.1 Clocking In Shift (Start of Workday)

At the beginning of your workday, click the blue Clock In Shift button at the top right of your portal.

  • The live Active Shift Timer starts running immediately (00:00:01...).
  • Your exact Clock In Time is captured in IST format (e.g. 09:00:00 AM).
  • The shift status badge turns green: SHIFT OPEN.
  • The system synchronizes your active presence to the Floor Shift Monitor for your team lead.
2.2 Taking & Resuming Breaks (Lunch, Tea, Personal)

Accurately recording breaks ensures your official on-clock Net Shift Hours are calculated properly:

  1. When stepping away, click the amber Take Break button. Status badge turns amber: ON BREAK.
  2. When returning to work, click Resume Shift. Badge returns to SHIFT OPEN.
  3. The break interval is logged in Break_Logs and added to your daily Total Break Duration scorecard.
2.3 Applying for Leave or Out-of-Office Permission

Click Apply Leave / Permission on the control bar:

  • Select Date: Pick today, a past missing timesheet date, or a future advance leave date.
  • Category: PL (Planned Leave), UL (Unplanned Leave), HL (Half Day - max 4.5 hrs net shift), Permission (1 to 3 hrs short permission), or OD (On Duty).
  • Attach Proof (Optional): Upload a Zoho applied screen shot
  • Submit: Click Submit Application.
Note on Short Permission: Submitting a short permission for today does NOT close your workday. You can continue clocking in, working, and logging tasks normally.
2.4 Clocking Out Shift (End of Workday)

At the conclusion of your shift:

  1. Click the red Clock Out Shift button.
  2. In the confirmation modal, review your Clock In, Clock Out, and calculated Total Net Worked Hours.
  3. If you took out-of-office permission earlier, optionally enter permission hours or attach gate pass proofs.
  4. Click Clock Out Shift Now. Your shift closes cleanly and the daily closed summary card appears.
Overnight Auto-Close Trigger: If you accidentally forget to clock out, the automated system cron (autoCloseOvernightShifts) safely finalizes your shift at midnight so the next day starts cleanly.
3 How to Record Daily Logs (Task Activity & Productivity)
Task Logging Guide

Associates record their productive output using the Log Task Activity form. The system dynamically computes elapsed time, earned benchmark hours, speed efficiency, and leakage loss in real time.

Form Fields Breakdown
  • Client & Project: Select your assigned client (e.g. iMerit, Innodata) and project.
  • Activity Type: Production Work, Idle / Downtime, or Bench / Internal Training.
  • AHT Benchmark (Mins/Unit): Auto-populated standard minutes allowed per unit.
  • Task Count (Units): Total physical items completed (images, documents, QA audits).
  • Start & End Timestamps: Work window timestamps (use the Now buttons for instant filling).
  • Task Status: COMPLETED, IN_PROGRESS, or BLOCKED.
  • Client Blocker (Mins): Delay spent waiting for client server fixes or query responses.
Real-Time Task Calculation Bar

As you fill in the form, the calculation bar previews your output live:

Net Worked: 3.00 hrs Earned: 3.00 hrs Est Speed: 100%

If a break occurred during the task window, the system automatically subtracts break minutes so your net task time is 100% accurate.

Mandatory Remarks Rule: If logging an entry with 0 Units (such as team meetings, tool training, or server downtime), you MUST provide a clear explanation in the Remarks / Notes field.

5 Operational Concepts: What is AHT, Leakage Time, Blocked Hour & Idle Time?
5.1 What is AHT (Average Handling Time)?

Average Handling Time (AHT) is the approved standard duration (in minutes) authorized to complete exactly one unit of productive work (e.g. annotating 1 image, checking 1 document, reviewing 1 audio segment).

Productive Earned Hours Formula:
Earned Hours = (Task Count Units × AHT Benchmark Mins) / 60

/* Example: If AHT = 12 mins and you complete 15 units: */
Earned Hours = (15 × 12) / 60 = 180 / 60 = 3.00 hrs
5.2 What is Leakage Time (Performance Leakage)?

Performance Leakage represents lost execution time when a billable task takes longer than the standard AHT benchmark without logged blockers. It measures the productivity gap between elapsed clock time and earned client value.

Performance Leakage Formula:
Leakage Hours = Max(0, Actual Worked Task Hours - Earned Hours - Blocked Hours)

Key Mathematical Rules:
• Overperforming (Faster than AHT): Leakage = 0.00 hrs (never negative).
• Client Blockers Deducted: Delays from client outages are subtracted so your speed is protected.
• Idle & Bench Exemption: Downtime and internal training are 100% exempt from leakage.
5.3 What is a Blocked Hour (Client Blocker / Query Wait)?

A Blocked Hour is productive time lost due to external client dependencies beyond the associate's control (e.g. client tool crash, waiting for query response from Client SME, missing batch data).

Blocked Hours Formula:
Blocked Hours = Blocked Minutes / 60

/* Example: Task took 4.00 hrs for 2.00 hrs earned, but you logged 60 mins (1.00 hr) tool blocker */
Leakage = 4.00 - 2.00 - 1.00 = 1.00 hr (instead of 2.00 hrs!)

6 Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

The platform features an automated midnight trigger (autoCloseOvernightShifts) that safety-closes unclosed shifts at midnight. The next morning, when you open the portal, click Clock In Shift to begin your new workday cleanly.

If the yellow Missing Timesheet Alert Banner appears at the top of your portal, click Back-Submit Entry. In the application modal, change the date picker to the target missing date, select your category (Present/Leave), and submit.

Leakage occurs when your actual task duration exceeded the standard AHT allowance. If the delay was caused by a client tool crash or waiting for client feedback, click the Edit (Pencil) icon on that task entry, input the delay into Client Blocker (Mins), and click Update. The blocker will be deducted from the leakage.

If file upload fails due to network connectivity, upload your proof document to your corporate Google Drive, copy the shareable view link, and paste it directly into the Or paste direct Drive link field before submitting.
