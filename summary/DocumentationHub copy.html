Executive Briefing & Architecture Report
Operational Scope: 80+ Employees Enterprise Operations & NetSuite-Style Time Tracking
1. Executive Summary & Scope

The Hurix Digital Attendance & Productivity Hub is a custom operational time tracking system built for 80+ team members across operations (Annotation, SME, XML Conversion, and Floor Management).

Core Scope: The platform delivers a robust, NetSuite-style time tracking structure natively inside Google Workspace, capturing shift clock-ins/outs, break pauses, task activity counts, and handling times tailored to Hurix Digital's operational workflows.

2. Strategic Objectives (Why This Tool)
NetSuite-Style Tracking

Provides enterprise-grade, NetSuite-like task breakdown and time allocation directly at the ground operational level.

Task-Level Precision

Captures unit counts, handling speeds (AHT), and active timestamps that standard high-level attendance tools cannot log.

Live Operations Control

Provides real-time visibility into team capacity, shift states, break durations, and production bottlenecks.

3. Billable & Non-Billable Activity Mechanics
Billable Client Production

Direct revenue-generating client work (e.g. iMerit, Innodata, Snorkel).

  • Credited with Earned Productive Hours based on approved AHT benchmarks.
  • Feeds client billing metrics and resource utilization ratios.
Non-Billable Internal Activities

Internal processes (e.g. Internal Training, Floor Management, Upskilling).

  • Tracked accurately for attendance and payroll without inflating billable client hours.
  • Keeps client metrics clean while recording legitimate operational effort.
4. Operational Metric Framework (Key Definitions)
Total Shift Net Hours

Net active shift time worked (Gross Shift Time minus total break pause duration).

Actual Worked Hours

Actual elapsed time spent performing specific production tasks during the shift.

Earned Productive Hours

Target output credit earned based on completed units and AHT benchmarks.

Performance Leakage Hours

Net variance between actual worked task time and earned output (Efficiency Gap).

Idle Hours

Unproductive downtime caused by waiting for task allocation or instructions.

Blocked Hours / Mins

Time hindered by external technical issues, tool outages, or client delays.

Bench Hours

Training or reserve capacity when staff are present but unassigned to client production.

Total Break Duration

Cumulative shift pause duration logged precisely via live break timers.

Earned Hours = Task Units × (AHT Benchmark Mins / 60)  |  Performance Leakage = Actual Worked Hours - Earned Hours

The application is accessible on any modern desktop or mobile web browser. User accounts are synchronized with Hurix Digital's central employee directory.

2 Employee Portal Guide (Daily Workflow)
All Staff
Step 2.1: Clocking In Shift (Start of Workday)

At the beginning of your workday, click the blue Clock In Shift button at the top of your portal.

  • The live shift timer starts immediately.
  • Your Clock In Time is recorded in IST format (e.g. 09:00:00 AM).
  • The shift status badge turns green: SHIFT OPEN.
Step 2.2: Taking & Resuming Breaks

When taking lunch, tea, or personal breaks:

  1. Click Take Break. The status badge turns amber: ON BREAK.
  2. When returning to work, click Resume Shift.
  3. The break duration is automatically added to your daily Total Break Duration scorecard.
Step 2.3: Logging Task Activities

As you work on production tasks throughout the day, fill out the Log Task Activity form on the left:

  1. Select Client (e.g., iMerit, Innodata), Project, and Activity Type.
  2. The system automatically populates the approved AHT Benchmark (Mins/Unit).
    LLM Complexity Exception: To accommodate standard-exceeding task complexity, selecting the LLM project dynamically unlocks the AHT Benchmark field. This allows manual adjustments to be logged, which automatically feeds into the live productivity and earned hours calculations.
  3. Enter Task Count (Units) completed.
  4. Set Work Start Time and Work End Time (or click the Now buttons).
  5. The live calculation bar instantly previews your Duration, Earned Hours, and Estimated Speed Efficiency.
  6. Click Log Productivity Entry.
Mandatory Remarks Rule: If logging a task entry with 0 Units (e.g., system downtime, meeting, training), you MUST enter mandatory details in the Remarks / Notes field.
Step 2.4: Applying for Leave or Permission

To apply for planned leave, unplanned leave, half day, or short permission:

  1. Click Apply Leave / Permission.
  2. Select Category: PL (Planned Leave), UL (Unplanned Leave), HL (Half Day), or Permission (1-3 hrs).
  3. For Permission or HL, enter the exact duration in hours (e.g., 2.0 hrs).
  4. Optionally attach a proof document (medical certificate, email approval) via file upload or Google Drive URL.
  5. Click Submit Application.
Note on Permission: Submitting a short permission or half-day request for today does NOT close your active shift. You can continue clocking in, working, and logging tasks normally.
Step 2.5: Clocking Out Shift (End of Workday)

At the end of your shift:

  1. Click the red Clock Out Shift button.
  2. In the confirmation modal, review your shift timings. If you took partial out-of-office time, enter Permission / Out-of-Office Hours.
  3. Optionally attach a Proof Document (gate pass, medical receipt, approval email) via local file upload or paste a Google Drive link.
  4. Click Clock Out Shift Now. Your daily summary scorecard updates and your shift closes cleanly.

Managers can update target AHT benchmarks for client activities:

  1. Click Manage Benchmarks.
  2. Select Client, Project, and Activity Type.
  3. Enter new AHT Benchmark in minutes and specify if the project is Billable.
  4. Click Save Benchmark. All future task entries immediately reflect the updated benchmark.
Step 3.4: Google Sheets Style Column Filters & Sorting

Managers and Team Leads can perform advanced data slice-and-dice audits directly on the Live Employee Shift & Productivity Monitor roster using interactive column-level controls:

  • Interactive Popovers: Click the funnel icon () next to any column header to trigger the filter and sort console, positioned dynamically below the column.
  • Roster Sorting: Sort the active table instantly by A-Z (Ascending) or Z-A (Descending) values (e.g., sort by Net Worked, earned hours, shift status, or names).
  • Condition Filtering: Define custom rule matches from the dropdown:
    • Text Rules: Contains, Does not contain, Starts with, Ends with, or exact Equals.
    • Blank Checks: Identify empty cells or incomplete data records using Is Empty or Is Not Empty.
    • Numerical Thresholds: Filter hours or minutes using Greater Than (>), Less Than (<), Greater or Equal (>=), etc.
  • Value Checklist: Deselect values dynamically using the checklist panel. Values matching empty entries are consolidated as (Blanks) so that rows with blank or empty columns are fully auditable. Use search to filter long checklists, or click Select All / Clear to reset selections.
  • Visual Indicators: Columns with active filter rules display a highlighted blue funnel background to indicate that a filter is applied to that specific metric.
  • Master Reset: Click the Clear Column Filters button (visible next to the status pills when any filter is active) to clear all column and sorting overrides.

1. Employee User Portal (UserView) Formulas & Business Logic
A. Shift Duration & Break Accumulation Engine (Net Shift Hours)

Calculates official on-clock Net Shift Worked Hours by deducting itemized break durations logged in Break_Logs:

1. Total Elapsed Shift Clock Time (ClockHours):
ClockHours = (ShiftEndTime - ShiftStartTime) / (1000 × 60 × 60)

2. Total Itemized Break Duration (BreakMins):
BreakMins = ∑ (BreakEnd - BreakStart) / (1000 × 60) /* Sum of all completed breaks matching log_id */

3. Official Shift Hours (Net Worked Hours):
Shift Net Hours (total_shift_hours) = Max(0, ClockHours - (BreakMins / 60)) /* Displayed everywhere as "Net Worked (Shift Hours)" */
B. Attendance, Leave & Shrinkage Rules

Preserves open shifts during temporary leaves/permissions and applies accurate shrinkage hours:

Shrinkage Hours Calculation:
• Full-Day Leave (PL / UL / ABSENT): 8.00 hrs | Shift Status = LEAVE_CLOSED | Net Shift Hours = 0.00 hrs
• Half-Day Leave (HL): 4.00 hrs | Net Shift Hours = Min(4.50, Clocked Shift Duration)
• Permission / On Duty (PERM / OD): PermissionHours + (BreakMins / 60) | Shift Status = OPEN
C. Activity & Task Log Calculations (Gross Task Hours Engine)

Differentiates billable production from non-billable training, idle downtime, and client blockers:

1. Billable Production Work:
• Gross Task Worked Hours (H_task_gross) = (TaskEndTime - TaskStartTime - AutoBreakMins) / 3,600,000
• Productive Earned Hours (H_earned) = (Task Count × AHT Benchmark Mins) / 60
• Speed Efficiency % = (H_earned / H_task_gross) × 100%
• Blocked Hours (H_blocked) = Blocked Mins / 60 /* Client Query / Waiting for Tool Fix */
• Performance Leakage Hours = Max(0, H_task_gross - H_earned - H_blocked) /* Blockers deducted from leakage */

2. Idle / Technical Downtime (Waiting for Allocation, Tool Outage, Server Crash):
• Idle Hours (H_idle) = H_task_gross
• Productive Earned Hours = 0.00 hrs | Speed Efficiency = 0%
• Performance Leakage Hours = 0.00 hrs /* 100% Exempt from leakage penalties */

3. Bench & Internal Upskilling (Training, Video AC Training, Bench):
• Bench Hours (H_bench) = H_task_gross
• Training Earned Credit = (Task Count × AHT Benchmark Mins) / 60
• Training Speed Efficiency % = (Training Earned Credit / H_bench) × 100%
• Billable Earned Hours = 0.00 hrs | Performance Leakage = 0.00 hrs /* Non-billable, zero leakage */

D. Floating-Point Precision & Rounding Standards

To eliminate binary floating-point rounding anomalies (e.g. .29999999998) and ensure consistent audit logs, a strict rounding standard is enforced across the system:

  • Roster and Ratios Display: All duration metrics—including Shift Net, Task Gross, Earned, Leakage, Idle, Blocked, and Bench hours—are formatted using .toFixed(2).
  • Break Duration Precision: Break duration aggregations for both individual rows and the global footer totals are formatted to exactly 2 decimal places in the UI to prevent floating-point accumulator noise.
  • Reporting Alignments: Spreadsheet storage retains full mathematical double-precision, but all user and executive exports (such as CSV files) are standardized to 2 decimal places to ensure reporting alignment.
2. Live Shift Monitoring Formulas (Admin Dashboard)

Aggregates real-time staff status across all active enrolled staff in User_Master:

1. Shift Status Classification:
• Clocked In = Count(shift_status ∈ {'OPEN', 'ON_BREAK', 'CLOSED'} OR attendance_status == 'PRESENT')
• On Break = Count(shift_status == 'ON_BREAK')
• Pending (Not Clocked In) = Count((shift_status == 'NOT_CLOCKED_IN' OR shift_status == '') AND attendance_status ∉ {'PL', 'UL', 'HL', 'PERMISSION', 'LEAVE_CLOSED'})
• On Leave = Count(shift_status == 'LEAVE_CLOSED' OR attendance_status ∈ {'PL', 'UL', 'HL', 'PERMISSION', 'OD'})

2. Team Shrinkage Rate %:
Team Shrinkage Rate = (OnLeaveCount / TotalEnrolledStaff) × 100%

3. Shift & Task Scorecard KPIs:
• Card 6: Net Worked (Shift Hours) = ∑ Attendance_Shift_Logs.total_shift_hours (Shift Net Active Time)
• Card 7: Gross Worked (Task Log Hours) = ∑ Project_Task_Logs.actual_worked_hours (Total Task Elapsed Time)
• Total Productive Earned Hours = ∑ ProductiveEarnedHours across all billable tasks
• Total Performance Leakage Hours = ∑ PerformanceLeakageHours
• Total Idle Hours = ∑ IdleHours | Total Bench Hours = ∑ BenchHours | Total Blocked Hours = ∑ BlockedHours

4. Overall Speed Efficiency %:
Overall Speed % = (Total Earned Hours / Total Task Gross Hours) × 100%

3. Executive Analytics & Period Aggregation Formulas

Aggregates multi-day performance across Month, Week, Date Range, Team, and Client filters:

1. Period Working Capacity (Staff-Days):
Period Working Days = Count(Monday-to-Friday Business Days in Selected Date Range)
Period Capacity (Staff-Days) = EnrolledStaffCount × PeriodWorkingDays

2. Period Attendance Rate %:
Period Attendance Rate = (Total Present Staff-Days / Period Capacity) × 100%

3. Period Shrinkage Rate %:
Period Shrinkage Rate = (Total Leave Staff-Days / Period Capacity) × 100%

4. Resource Utilization Rate %:
Resource Utilization Rate = (Total Gross Task Hours / Total Shift Net Worked Hours) × 100%

5. Billing Realization Rate %:
Billing Realization Rate = (Total Earned Hours / Total Shift Net Worked Hours) × 100%

6. Speed Efficiency Index %:
Speed Efficiency Index = (Total Earned Hours / Total Gross Task Hours) × 100%

7. Leakage Loss Rate %:
Leakage Loss Rate = (Total Performance Leakage Hours / Total Shift Net Worked Hours) × 100%
Strict Leave & Future Date Isolation Engine

Guarantees zero phantom hour distortion across past, present, and advance leave filings:

Strict Calculation Rules:
• Full-Day Leave (PL, UL, ABSENT, LEAVE_CLOSED): Shift Net Hours = 0.00 hrs (Timestamp parsing suppressed).
• Half-Day Leave (HL): Shift Net Hours = Min(4.50, Clocked Shift Duration).
• Future Dates (Date > Today): Shift Net Hours = 0.00 hrs | Task Logs = Quarantined.
• Default Filter State: Executive Analytics defaults strictly to Today's Date. Month/Week/Custom selection switches to cumulative aggregation.
Dimension Matrix Formulas (Client, Project, Team, Manager)
Dimension Metric Mathematical Formula Business Purpose Attendance % (Present Staff / Enrolled Staff) × 100% Evaluates headcount availability across teams and projects. Utilization % (Billable Hours / Total Worked Hours) × 100% Measures client-facing productive allocation vs. bench overhead. Shrinkage % (Leave Staff-Days / Total Capacity) × 100% Calculates floor absenteeism and operational capacity loss. AHT Variance % ((Actual AHT - Benchmark AHT) / Benchmark AHT) × 100% Identifies whether client benchmarks are realistic or need recalibration.

The Hurix Digital Productivity & Time Tracking Portal replaces manual timesheets with automated, AHT-benchmarked operational tracking. It delivers 360-degree real-time visibility into staff attendance, active shift timings, productive earned credit hours, break durations, and performance leakage.

  • Automated Productive Earned Credit: Calculated per task unit based on client target Average Handling Time (AHT).
  • Real-Time Time Leakage Loss Tracking: Instantly highlights output gaps between shift hours worked vs. standard earned credit.
  • Absence vs Permission Separation: Explicit operational distinction between Full/Half Day Leaves and 1–2 hour short permissions.

Comprehensive Field & Metric Dictionary

1. User Portal View (UserView.html)

A. User View Scorecards & KPI Meters (What For & Logic)
Card / KPI Name What It Is For (Purpose) Business Logic & Calculation
Active Shift Duration Timer Provides associates live visibility into their elapsed shift time on the floor today. Live Shift Time = (Current Timestamp - Clock In Timestamp) - Total Breaks Taken. Increments every second in real time.
Break Duration (userBreakMins) Displays total break minutes accumulated today to maintain compliance with standard floor pause limits. Sum of all closed break durations plus active break timer from Break_Logs.
Net Worked Hours Shows total attendance duration credited for today's shift. (Shift End - Shift Start) - (Total Break Minutes / 60). (Locks upon Clock Out).
Logged Task Hours Total time spent across all completed task entries submitted today. ∑ (Task End Time - Task Start Time) across all rows in the task log table.
Earned Hours Total standard billable output credit generated from task deliverables. (Units Completed × AHT Benchmark Mins) / 60. (Bench tasks earn 0.00 hrs).
Performance Leakage Highlights any pacing deficit between actual task time spent and earned benchmark output. Max(0, Task Duration - Earned Hours - Blocked Hours). Approved blockers are deducted.
Speed Efficiency % Visual performance pacing badge (⚡ $\ge 100\%$, ⏱️ $80-99\%$, 🐢 $<80\%$). Round((Earned Hours / Logged Task Hours) × 100)%.
B. Task Entry Form & Log Table — Field-by-Field Master Dictionary

Every field in the task logging interface, why it exists, and how associates must populate it:

Field Name Input Type Why It Exists (Operational Purpose) How to Enter (User Guidelines)
Active Client (client) Dropdown Identifies the client account for billing, financial reporting, and benchmark binding. Select the assigned client (e.g. iMerit, Innodata, Hurix). Auto-defaults from user profile.
Active Project (project) Dropdown Links the work to a specific project workflow and automatically pulls the approved AHT benchmark. Select from the dynamic project dropdown associated with the selected client.

2. Admin Shift Monitor View (AdminView.html)

A. Admin Floor KPIs (Description, Business Logic & Exact Formulas)
Scorecard KPI Description & Purpose Business Logic Exact Formula
TOTAL RESOURCES Total active staff enrolled in the operational workforce directory. Counts all active users in User_Master (excluding non-shift Super Admins). Total Resources = 78
CLOCKED IN Number of associates currently present on shift for the selected date. Unique staff count with shift status OPEN, ON_BREAK, or CLOSED. Count(Shift Status ∈ {OPEN, ON_BREAK, CLOSED})
PENDING Associates who have not yet clocked in or submitted approved leave. Total floor capacity minus clocked-in staff and approved leave staff. Total (78) - Clocked In (54) - On Leave (12) = 12
ON LEAVE (Shrink %) Staff on approved Planned (PL), Unplanned (UL), Half Day (HL), or Absent. Sum of all approved leave instances and calculated floor shrinkage percentage. Leaves = PL + UL + (0.5 × HL) + ABSENT
Shrink % = (Leaves / 78) × 100
ON BREAK Associates currently away on an active paused break. Real-time count of staff with shift status ON_BREAK. Count(Shift Status = 'ON_BREAK')
NET WORKED Cumulative shift attendance hours worked across all clocked-in staff. Sum of live running shift hours (or closed shift hours) minus all break minutes. ∑ ((Shift End - Shift Start) - (Break Mins / 60))
GROSS WORKED Cumulative time spent on logged production and bench tasks. Sum of actual task duration across all submitted task logs today. ∑ actual_worked_hours
LEAKAGE LOSS Total operational output deficit across the entire floor today. Sum of performance leakage hours across all associates. ∑ Max(0, Task Duration - Earned - Blocked)
BLOCKED / BENCH Total non-billable capacity (Bench/Training + Tool Outages/Downtime). Cumulative sum of Blocked Hours, Bench Hours, and Idle Downtime Hours. ∑ (Blocked Hours + Bench Hours + Idle Hours)

3. Executive Analytics View (ExecutiveSummaryView.html)

A. Executive KPIs (What For, Business Logic & Formulas)
Executive KPI What It Is For & Why It Matters Business Logic Exact Formula
TOTAL RESOURCES Establishes the active resource capacity base over the selected period. Single-day view strictly equals 78; Multi-day equals total employee-day instances. Single Day = 78
Period = 78 × Period Days
CLOCKED IN (Att %) Measures workforce attendance consistency and operational reporting rate. Total present days logged across the period and percentage against capacity. Att % = (Present Days / Total Staff Instances) × 100
PENDING Highlights unrecorded workdays requiring manager timesheet reconciliation. Capacity minus present days minus approved leave days. Pending = Total Staff - Present Days - Leave Days
ON LEAVE (Shrink %) Evaluates capacity lost to planned/unplanned leaves to guide floor staffing buffers. Sum of all PL, UL, HL, and Absent days over total capacity instances. Shrink % = (Total Leaves / Total Staff Instances) × 100
NET WORKED Total operational floor labor hours invested across the filtered period. Cumulative shift attendance hours across all associates. ∑ ((Shift End - Shift Start) - (Breaks / 60))
GROSS WORKED Total active task effort logged into production workflows. Cumulative task hours recorded in Project_Task_Logs. ∑ actual_worked_hours
EARNED HOURS Total billable standard production output revenue generated for clients. Sum of standard output credit earned from completed units $\times$ AHT. ∑ ((Task Count × Benchmark AHT) / 60)
LEAKAGE LOSS Financial and operational cost of productivity gaps across delivery teams. Sum of task execution leakage after deducting approved system blockers. ∑ Max(0, Task Duration - Earned - Blocked)
