Executive Summary & Business Value

The Hurix Digital Productivity Portal replaces manual timesheets with automated, AHT-benchmarked operational tracking.It delivers 360-degree real-time visibility into staff attendance, active shift timings, productive earned credit hours, break durations, and performance leakage.

  • Automated Productive Earned Credit: Standard hours calculated ($\text{Units} \times \text{Target AHT}$).
  • Performance Leakage Tracking: Highlights output gaps vs. worked shift time.
  • Leave vs Permission Separation: Leaves (`PL`/`UL`/`HL`) are 100% separated from Short Permissions (`PERM`).

PROMPT ENGINEERING WORKFLOW
How Gemini AI Was Used via Structured Prompts
Google DeepMind Gemini AI

The entire Hurix Productivity Hub was developed through interactive Prompt-Driven AI Engineering. Rather than manual coding from scratch, natural language prompts instructed Google DeepMind Gemini to design, code, debug, and refine every component:

Prompt Step 1: Requirements

Prompted Gemini with floor operation requirements: 80+ staff across Annotation, SME, and XML teams, NetSuite-style time tracking, and AHT speed credit formulas.

Prompt Step 2: Server Logic

Instructed Gemini to generate Google Apps Script backend handlers (`Code.gs`) for AHT earned credit, leakage calculations, and automated weekly email digests.

Prompt Step 3: Executive UI

Prompted Gemini to build ultra-sleek, glassmorphic interfaces with single-row 8 KPI scorecards, frozen 1st column sticky tables, and multi-role dashboards.

Prompt Step 4: Refinement

Iteratively prompted Gemini to disentangle leave hours from short permission hours, auto-close overnight shifts, and sanitize inputs against XSS.
